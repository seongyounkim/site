About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Seoul National University, advised by Jun Ho Whang and Gye-Seon Lee.

My work lies at the intersection of geometry, topology, representation theory, and dynamics.

Research

I study character varieties and skein algebras of surfaces, mapping class groups, and geometric structures on manifolds. Current themes include compactifications of moduli spaces, algebraic and dynamical rigidity, and arithmetic aspects of character varieties.
